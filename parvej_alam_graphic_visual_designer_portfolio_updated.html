Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What design services can I enquire about?

Logo and brand identity concepts, social media creatives, posters, flyers, print layouts, and promotional graphics.

How do I get a price?

Send the design type, dimensions or platform, quantity, deadline, and any reference material through WhatsApp or email. A quote can then be discussed.

Are the projects shown real client projects?

The current cards are labelled concept samples for portfolio demonstration. They should not be treated as verified client commissions.

Do you use AI in design?

AI-assisted ideation and prompt writing are included as skills. Any AI-generated material should be reviewed for accuracy, originality, usage rights, and suitability before delivery.

Can I request revisions?

Revision scope should be agreed before a project starts, based on the brief and quote.
